# Hong Kong privacy wording (use these phrases, nothing stronger)

- Hong Kong's Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance expects people to be told why and how their personal data is collected (a personal information collection statement, PICS).
- PCPD recommends that a website makes its privacy policy statement available.
- Hong Kong has no cookie-specific law. The Ordinance applies where tracking data counts as personal data. PCPD has guidance on online tracking (recommended practice, not a statute).

Report what the site does ("analytics loads on every visit; the privacy page does not mention analytics"). Never write "illegal", "compliant", "you will be fined" or "protects you from complaints". For a real compliance question, tell the user to check PCPD's own pages or a lawyer.
